🇨🇩 DRC — Ituri, Nord-Kivu, South Kivu ACTIVE OUTBREAK
May 23
BREAKINGWHO upgrades DRC risk level to VERY HIGH — the highest national-level classification. Tedros: "The Ebola outbreak in DRC is spreading rapidly. The situation is deeply worrisome." Nearly 750 suspected cases and 177 suspected deaths. 82 confirmed cases, 7 confirmed deaths. Tedros: "We know the epidemic in DRC is much larger." Al Jazeera ABC News
May 23
UN releases $60 million from CERF (Central Emergency Response Fund) to accelerate the Ebola response in DRC and the region — the largest single emergency fund release for this outbreak. PBS NewsHour
May 23
Hospital torched in Ituri by angry residents after authorities refused to release the body of a local football player who died of suspected Ebola. Ituri provincial government bans all public gatherings to stop spread. WHO: "violence and insecurity are impeding the response." Community misinformation and distrust of outside authorities identified as a primary driver of uncontrolled transmission. CIDRAP UN News
May 23
CDC deploys 20 epidemiologists to DRC and 23 to Uganda; 7 additional subject-matter experts being deployed across both countries. CDC incident manager Satish Pillai briefs at US CDC press conference. WHO recommends 2 monoclonal antibodies be prioritised for Bundibugyo clinical trials — obeldesivir (antiviral) also recommended for assessment as post-exposure prophylaxis for high-risk contacts. CIDRAP
May 23
ECDC publishes updated risk assessment: 650+ suspected cases, 64 confirmed, 160 deaths as of their latest data. Over 1,000 contacts under follow-up in Ituri. 13 new confirmed cases since May 19 update (12 Ituri + 1 Nord-Kivu). ECDC assesses EU/EEA risk as "very low." ECDC
May 23
NEWKisangani (Tshopo Province) emerges as new area of concern. The South Kivu patient — a 28-year-old man — had travelled from Kisangani before dying undiagnosed in Bukavu. Though Tshopo has no confirmed cases yet, Kisangani is one of the DRC's busiest transport hubs, raising alarm about undetected chains of transmission. Xinhua
May 23
Community trust now a critical barrier. DRC Health Minister Roger Kamba confirmed the alert was delayed because some residents believed the illness was "mystical." INRB head Jean-Jacques Muyembe warned: "When people see that instructions are announced by people from their own area, they believe them. If it is someone from Kinshasa, they doubt." He stressed that building community trust is now the primary challenge. Xinhua
May 23
M23 rebels, who control South Kivu (Bukavu fell in February 2025) and part of North Kivu (Goma), stated they are committed to working with international partners to contain the outbreak, though the response has been complicated by the virus appearing in densely populated urban areas. M23 has never managed a serious epidemic response. Al Jazeera
May 22
BREAKINGEbola confirmed in South Kivu — 3rd DRC province. The Bukavu case involved a person coming from Kisangani who died before diagnosis was confirmed. Bukavu fell to M23 in February 2025. Rwanda closes DRC border. Al Jazeera
May 22
BREAKINGWHO revises vaccine timeline to 9 months — major revision from 2-month estimate. No ring vaccination possible. Response relies entirely on isolation, contact tracing, safe burials, supportive care. GBC Ghana
May 22
India-Africa Forum Summit postponed indefinitely (was May 28–31, 55 African nations in New Delhi). India activates Ebola screening at Delhi IGI and Hyderabad airports. AP/ABC Bloomberg
May 22
Aid groups in Bunia warn outbreak is "gaining momentum" with dire supply shortage. Hama Amado (Alima): "We are still far from saying the situation is under control." Nearly 20 tons of aid airlifted but doctors using out-of-date masks in general wards — no isolation space. 920,000+ IDPs in Ituri. AP
May 22
Index case confirmed: a nurse who developed symptoms April 24 and died 3 days later. After her death, family handled the body for burial without PPE — identified as the primary amplification event. ICT
May 22
Critical treatment gap: Inmazeb, Ebanga (antibodies) and Ervebo (vaccine) all target Zaire ebolavirus only — not Bundibugyo. Dr Stafford is receiving experimental off-label antibodies. No approved countermeasure exists for this strain. Mappr
May 21
WHO press conference: 600+ suspected, 51 confirmed, 139 deaths — 3rd largest Ebola outbreak on record. Numbers expected to keep rising. MRC London: true total may exceed 1,000. WHO
May 20
PHECSAfrica CDC declares continental emergency. South Africa pledges $2.5M. M23 rebels guard INRB Goma lab. Ebola confirmed in Butembo and Goma (Nord-Kivu). Al Jazeera
May 17
PHEICWHO declares PHEIC — first ever without Emergency Committee pre-consultation. DRC cabinet emergency session. WHO CFE approves $3.9M. WHO.int
May 15
DRC MoH officially confirms 17th outbreak. Bundibugyo strain. 4 HCW deaths in 4 days at Mongbwalu. INRB confirms in 8/13 samples. US CDC
Apr 20
First suspected death retrospectively identified. Virus circulated undetected ~2 months. Co-circulating arboviruses and wrong test kits (Zaire) caused 4-week detection gap. Science AAAS

NEWUgandans who attended a burial in eastern DRC returned home with Ebola symptoms. Taken to Fort Portal (western Uganda) for treatment — first Ebola development outside Kampala, suggesting new transmission chain linked to DRC burial. Wikipedia

UPDATEWHO field rep Dr Ancia from Bunia: "There is still very low contact tracing in Ituri, particularly the city of Bunia, but there was better contact tracing happening in North Kivu." Uganda situation described as "stable" by WHO — 2 confirmed cases with no further local spread confirmed. ABC News

US DHS Dulles requirement applies to all passengers including US citizens. US Embassy strictly limiting official travel to western Uganda to mission-critical only. Secretary Rubio: "Number one priority is making sure it doesn't come to the United States." CNN

Dr Peter Stafford: "I was really concerned I wasn't going to make it. Now I'm cautiously optimistic." Receiving experimental monoclonal antibodies at Charite Berlin. Wife and 4 children in Berlin seeing him through window. Dr LaRochelle at Bulovka Prague — asymptomatic. CBS ABC
May 20
Museveni postpones Martyrs Day (2M attendees) at WHO DG request. Nationwide ban: handshakes, hugs, unnecessary physical contact. Busunga border crossing active screening. No local transmission from original 2 Kampala cases. US Embassy UG

As of May 22: outbreak has spread from Ituri to Butembo, Goma (Nord-Kivu), and South Kivu. Now also in Kampala and Fort Portal (Uganda). WHO believes outbreak started ~April 20 and spread undetected ~2 months. MRC London estimates true case count may already exceed 1,000. Goma and South Kivu are under M23 rebel control, severely complicating response.

The 2026 Bundibugyo outbreak has become the 3rd largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded in under 2 weeks of being officially declared. With an estimated true case count possibly exceeding 1,000 (MRC London), it could rise further. Unlike all large prior outbreaks, this one has no vaccine and no approved treatment.
