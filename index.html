BREAKINGEbola confirmed in South Kivu province — the outbreak has now spread to a third DRC province. Rwanda has closed its border with DRC in response. South Kivu is controlled by Rwanda-backed M23 rebels. The spread to South Kivu means the outbreak now covers Ituri, Nord-Kivu, and South Kivu — a vast area of eastern DRC. Al Jazeera

BREAKINGWHO revises vaccine timeline to NINE MONTHS — a major upward revision from the 2-month estimate given days ago. This significantly changes the response calculus: there will be no vaccine-assisted ring vaccination for the foreseeable future. Response relies entirely on surveillance, isolation, contact tracing, safe burials, and supportive care. GBC Ghana / WHO The Week India

India-Africa Forum Summit postponed indefinitely. Summit scheduled May 28–31 in New Delhi (leaders from 55 African nations) cancelled due to "evolving health situation in parts of Africa." India also activates Ebola screening at Delhi IGI and Hyderabad airports for travelers from DRC, Uganda, and South Sudan. India reaffirms solidarity with Africa and readiness to support Africa CDC response. ABC News / AP Bloomberg
May 22
On-the-ground reality in Bunia: Despite nearly 20 tons of aid being airlifted, doctors are treating suspected Ebola patients in general wards using out-of-date facemasks because there is no isolation space. Over 920,000 internally displaced people in Ituri province alone. WHO Dr Ancia: "The more we investigate, the more we realise the outbreak has already disseminated across borders and into other provinces." AP GBC Ghana
After her death, family members handled and prepared the body for burial without protective precautions — this unprotected burial is identified as the primary amplification event. This explains how the outbreak accelerated before any Ebola was suspected. Infection Control Today
May 22
KEYCritical treatment gap confirmed: The two licensed Ebola monoclonal antibodies (Inmazeb, Ebanga) and the Ervebo vaccine all target Zaire ebolavirus only — not Bundibugyo. The response relies entirely on isolation, contact tracing, safe burials, and supportive care (fluids, electrolytes, oxygen). Dr Stafford is receiving experimental off-label antibodies. No approved countermeasure exists for this strain. Mappr ICT

NEWAid groups warn outbreak is "gaining momentum" and they are in dire need of supplies and staff. We are still far from saying the situation is under control." AP / Washington Times

UNICEF
Coordinating PPE and hygiene supply pipelines to border states; mobilising community health workers for risk communication; managing support for 11,000+ South Sudanese refugees in Ituri. Explicitly flags South Sudan requires higher international support than Uganda given no prior EVD experience. UN News
IOM
Monitoring cross-border population movement at South Sudan–DRC borders. Nearly 300,000 displaced in Jonglei; 1.35 million people lost healthcare access after 26 health facilities destroyed. Supporting PoE screening assessments at Nimule and Gangura crossings. ReliefWeb
OCHA
Coordinating humanitarian response environment with active cholera outbreak (102,000+ cases, 1,662 deaths), conflict restricting access in Jonglei and Upper Nile. Any Ebola case would arrive in an already overwhelmed health system. OCHA flags dangerous compound crisis. ReliefWeb
IRC
IRC Senior Health Coordinator Dr Lievin Bangali (DRC): "Communities in eastern DRC are already facing immense pressure from conflict, displacement, and a collapsing health system. Years of underfunding, compounded by recent cuts, have weakened the ability to detect and respond to outbreaks quickly." Same systemic risks apply to South Sudan border areas. IRC
SS Gov
Ministry of Health activated PHEOC; multi-sectoral taskforce active; screening at Juba Airport; isolation units at Juba Teaching Hospital and UNMISS Level II; national EVD hotline reactivated (MTN and Zain). High-risk border states: Yei River, Torit, Maridi, Gbudue, Tambura.
Key gap
No Bundibugyo vaccine or treatment. WHO: a vaccine candidate possibly 2 months away. South Sudan cannot use ring vaccination. Containment depends entirely on surveillance and IPC in a health system simultaneously managing a cholera crisis and active armed conflict. WHO DON
