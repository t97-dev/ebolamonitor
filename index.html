🇨🇩 DRC — Ituri, Nord-Kivu, South Kivu ACTIVE OUTBREAK
May 23
NEWKisangani (Tshopo Province) emerges as new area of concern. The South Kivu patient — a 28-year-old man — had travelled from Kisangani before dying undiagnosed in Bukavu. Though Tshopo has not seen any confirmed cases, Kisangani is one of the DRC's busiest transport hubs, raising alarm about undetected chains of transmission. Xinhua Al Jazeera
May 23
Community trust now a critical barrier. DRC Health Minister Roger Kamba confirmed the alert was delayed because some residents believed the illness was "mystical." INRB head Jean-Jacques Muyembe warned: "When people see that instructions are announced by people from their own area, they believe them. If it is someone from Kinshasa, they doubt." He stressed that building community trust is now the primary challenge. Xinhua
May 23
M23 rebels, who control South Kivu (Bukavu fell in February 2025) and part of North Kivu (Goma), stated they are committed to working with international partners to contain the outbreak, though the response has been complicated by the virus appearing in densely populated urban areas. M23 has never managed a serious epidemic response. Al Jazeera
May 22
BREAKINGEbola confirmed in South Kivu — 3rd DRC province. The Bukavu case involved a person coming from Kisangani who died before diagnosis was confirmed. Bukavu fell to M23 in February 2025. Rwanda closes DRC border. Al Jazeera
May 22
BREAKINGWHO revises vaccine timeline to 9 months — major revision from 2-month estimate. No ring vaccination possible. Response relies entirely on isolation, contact tracing, safe burials, supportive care. GBC Ghana
May 22
India-Africa Forum Summit postponed indefinitely (was May 28–31, 55 African nations in New Delhi). India activates Ebola screening at Delhi IGI and Hyderabad airports. AP/ABC Bloomberg
May 22
Aid groups in Bunia warn outbreak is "gaining momentum" with dire supply shortage. Hama Amado (Alima): "We are still far from saying the situation is under control." Nearly 20 tons of aid airlifted but doctors using out-of-date masks in general wards — no isolation space. 920,000+ IDPs in Ituri. AP
May 22
Index case confirmed: a nurse who developed symptoms April 24 and died 3 days later. After her death, family handled the body for burial without PPE — identified as the primary amplification event. ICT
May 22

As of May 22: outbreak has spread from Ituri to Butembo, Goma (Nord-Kivu), and South Kivu. Now also in Kampala and Fort Portal (Uganda). WHO believes outbreak started ~April 20 and spread undetected ~2 months. MRC London estimates true case count may already exceed 1,000. Goma and South Kivu are under M23 rebel control, severely complicating response.

The 2026 Bundibugyo outbreak has become the 3rd largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded in under 2 weeks of being officially declared. With an estimated true case count possibly exceeding 1,000 (MRC London), it could rise further. Unlike all large prior outbreaks, this one has no vaccine and no approved treatment.
