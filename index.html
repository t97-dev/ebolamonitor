May 2026 — declaration & emergency response
May 29
WHO DON 605: 134 confirmed (incl. 9 Uganda), 18 confirmed deaths; DRC 906 suspected, 223 suspected deaths. US expands airport screening to 4 airports (Dulles, Atlanta, Houston, JFK); $300M via OCHA. Panama and Australia issue alerts. WHO DON 605
May 27
Uganda closes its border with DRC for 4 weeks (21-day isolation for DRC arrivals). US State Dept announces $112M bilateral assistance. Wikipedia
May 25
Two suspected cases hospitalised in Milan, Italy (travellers from Uganda). DRC football team isolating in Belgium. Outbreak now spans an area larger than Florida. ECDC
May 22
WHO upgrades DRC risk to VERY HIGH. Rwanda closes DRC border. Vaccine timeline revised to 9 months. IHR Emergency Committee temporary recommendations issued — naming South Sudan, Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania, Sudan, Burundi. WHO DON 603
May 21
WHO: 3rd-largest outbreak on record. South Kivu case confirmed (imported from Tshopo/Kisangani). UK announces up to £20M. No vaccine/treatment exists for Bundibugyo. WHO
May 20
Africa CDC declares continental emergency (PHECS). 600 cases / 139 suspected deaths. Butembo and Goma (North Kivu) confirmed. South Africa pledges $2.5M. Al Jazeera
May 18
WHO: DRC 516 suspected, 131 deaths + 33 confirmed, 4 confirmed deaths, across 4 Ituri + 3 North Kivu health zones. Uganda: 2 confirmed, 1 death. US begins restricting entry for travellers from DRC/Uganda/South Sudan (past 21 days). Wikipedia
May 16–17
WHO declares a PHEIC — first ever without Emergency Committee pre-consultation. CDC initiates a public health emergency response. WHO approves $3.9M CFE. WHO
May 15
DRC officially declares its 17th Ebola outbreak (246 suspected cases, 80 deaths, 4 among confirmed) in Ituri. Uganda declares an outbreak the same day after an imported case sought care in Kampala. INRB confirms Bundibugyo in 8 samples. ReliefWeb
May 14
INRB (Kinshasa) confirms 8 cases of a non-Zaire filovirus; genomic sequencing classifies it as Bundibugyo ebolavirus. Wikipedia
May 5
WHO alerted to a high-mortality illness in Mongbwalu Health Zone, Ituri — including deaths among health workers. Response team dispatched. WHO
April 2026 — origin
Apr 30
First outbreak signal reported. Initial testing in Bunia is negative for Zaire Ebola (wrong-strain kits); samples sent to INRB Kinshasa — contributing to a multi-week detection gap. Wikipedia
~Apr 24
Index case: a nurse in Mongbwalu develops symptoms; died Apr 27. Family handled the body without protection — the primary amplification event. Early infections may date to Jan–Feb 2026. UN News
Dec 2025
The previous DRC Ebola outbreak (Zaire strain, 16th) is declared over — just five months before this 17th outbreak begins. CDC History
